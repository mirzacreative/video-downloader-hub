Frequently asked questions

Is this video downloader free?

Yes. No signup and no payment.

Can I download TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat videos without a watermark?

Yes — with the “Remove watermark” switch on, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat videos are fetched from the clean source file when one is available, so no moving logo is burned in. Availability depends on the video and on each platform’s current systems.

Which resolutions can I choose?

It depends on the source video — typically 1080p, 720p, 480p and 360p, plus an audio-only option where available.

Why does a link sometimes fail?

Private videos, login-only content and videos from accounts that block downloads cannot be fetched. Platforms also change their systems often, which can temporarily break extraction.

Please use responsibly

Only download videos you have the right to download — your own content, or content the owner has made available for download. Respect creators, platform terms and copyright law. This tool does not host any videos; it fetches publicly available content on request.
